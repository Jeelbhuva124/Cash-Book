 The objective of the project Glowify  – An Online Cosmetics and 
Fragrance Platform is to design and develop a professional, responsive, 
and user -friendly e -commerce website for selling cosmetics, skincare, 
hair care, and fragrance products. 
 The platform offers essential features such as produ ct browsing, 
shopping cart, wishlist, order placement, and tracking for customers, 
along with a secure admin panel to manage products, categories, users, 
and orders. 
 The aim is to deliver a functional and scalable system that simulates real -
world online retail experiences in the cosmetics and fragrance domain. 
 
 
Start To End Process 
1. Project Planning & Requirement Analysis 
 Define the purpose of the website: selling cosmetics, skincare, hair 
care, and fragrances online. 
 Identify key features for users and admins such as product browsing, 
cart and wishlist management, secure login, order placement, and 
admin product management. 
 Finalize the technology stack: React, CSS, JavaScript, Bootstrap 
(frontend), Node, and Mongodb (database). 
2. UI/UX Design 
 Design wireframes or layout sketches for all major pages: Homepage, 
Product Details, Cart, Wishlist, Login/Register, User Dashboard, and 
Admin Panel. 
 Ensure a modern, clean, and responsive interface. 
 Use a consistent color scheme, readable fonts, and high -quality 
product images to enhance visual appeal.
